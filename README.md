One haiku about debugging, three lines in the 5-7-5 syllable shape, 66 characters: a breakpoint glows, the watch window lies, and the last line lands the turn — it was a typo. The haiku lives in `entry.txt`; `main.py` copies it byte-for-byte to the output as `/output/entry.txt`. The haiku was picked by a candidate tournament: eight candidates were syllable-counted (six held the 5-7-5 shape), then judged pairwise by the bounty's own judge model, z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, against the published judge brief copied word for word — the winner took 9 of 10 games in the main round and a 5-1 tiebreak against the runner-up.

## Run it

mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
